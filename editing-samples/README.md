# Editing Samples

This folder contains editing samples that demonstrate my ability to improve clarity, concision, readability, and overall effectiveness while preserving the original meaning and intent of the writing.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Copyediting
- Clarity and concision
- Grammar and sentence structure
- Removing unnecessary wording
- Improving readability
- Maintaining the writer's original meaning
- Editorial decision-making
- Explaining the reasoning behind edits

## Editing Approach

My goal when editing is to make writing clearer, more concise, and easier to understand while maintaining the original voice, meaning, and purpose. I focus on making intentional changes that improve the reader's experience rather than changing wording simply for the sake of changing it.
