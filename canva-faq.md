## What is Canva?

Canva is an online graphic design platform that enables users to create visual content such as presentations, social media graphics, flyers, posters, and marketing materials.

Canva provides both free and paid design tools and can be accessed through a web browser or mobile application.

---

## How do I create a new design?

### Steps

1. Sign in to your Canva account.
2. Select **Create a Design** from the home page.
3. Choose a design type or enter custom dimensions.
4. Begin editing your design using Canva's tools and templates.

### Result

A new blank or template-based project opens in the Canva editor.

---

## How do I use a template?

### Steps

1. Open a new or existing design.
2. Select the **Design** tab.
3. Browse available templates.
4. Click a template to apply it.
5. Replace placeholder text and images with your own content.

### Note

Applying a template may overwrite existing page layouts.

---

## How do I upload my own images?

### Steps

1. Open the Canva editor.
2. Select **Uploads** from the navigation menu.
3. Click **Upload Files**.
4. Select the image or file from your device.
5. Wait for the upload to complete.

### Supported Content

- Images
- Videos
- Audio files

Uploaded files remain available within your Canva account for future projects.

---

## How do I share a design with others?

### Steps

1. Open the design you want to share.
2. Select **Share** in the upper-right corner.
3. Enter the recipient's email address or generate a shareable link.
4. Choose the appropriate permission level:
   - Viewer
   - Commenter
   - Editor
5. Select **Send**.

### Result

Recipients receive access according to the permissions assigned.

---

## How do I download a design?

### Steps

1. Open the completed design.
2. Select **Share**.
3. Click **Download**.
4. Choose the desired file format.
5. Select **Download** again.

### Common File Types

| Format | Recommended Use |
|----------|----------------|
| PNG | Images and graphics |
| JPG | Smaller image files |
| PDF Standard | General document sharing |
| PDF Print | High-quality printing |
| MP4 | Video content |

## How do I recover a deleted design?

If a design was recently deleted:

1. Navigate to the Canva home page.
2. Open **Trash**.
3. Locate the deleted design.
4. Select **Restore**.

### Note

Designs permanently removed from the Trash folder may not be recoverable.

---

## Why is Canva running slowly?

Performance issues may occur due to browser limitations, internet connectivity, or large design files.

### Recommended Actions

- Refresh the browser page.
- Clear browser cache and cookies.
- Close unused browser tabs.
- Check internet connectivity.
- Try a different supported browser.
- Reduce the number of high-resolution assets in the design.

If issues persist, contact Canva Support.
